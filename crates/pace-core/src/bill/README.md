# `bill`

The parts of `bill.rs`. `invoice.rs` shows a written invoice again: the bill of
the entries billed on one reference, from the amounts their `billing.marked`
events froze, and the client a reference names. `bill.rs` builds the bill of a
client over a period and puts the lines of both together.
